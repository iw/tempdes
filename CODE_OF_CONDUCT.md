# Code of conduct

tempdes follows the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct).
It sets out the minimum behaviour expected from everyone who takes part in the project, in
issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

In short:

* be friendly and patient;
* be welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience;
* be considerate and respectful;
* be careful with your words;
* when you disagree, try to understand why.

Harassment and exclusionary behaviour aren't acceptable.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, [@iw](https://github.com/iw),
privately rather than replying in a public thread. The contact details are on their GitHub
profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code of conduct. They may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour
is harmful to the community.
